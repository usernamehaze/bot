Cursor Cassie

A cursor that came alive. Designed & animated in code.

state: idle
01

Construction

She starts as the Cassie pointer — the 7-point arrow from the logo. Every corner gets its own radius: big and soft outside, small fillets in the two notches. Her tail is cut out and hinged, so it can wag. Her eyes are text carets.

02

Expressions

Her moods borrow from the cursor's own vocabulary: an I-beam while you type, an hourglass when she's busy, ⊘ when something goes wrong, a highlighter stripe and marching ants when she highlights and snips. Tap any to play it big.

03

Accessories

Hats sit on her tip at −28°, glasses on her caret eyes. Some are just for here: a salakot for Independence Day, a sampaguita, and a Santa hat for the long Philippine "ber months" Christmas.

04

State machine

In the app, every moment maps to a state. She changes on her own — tap a row to see it.

Cursor Cassie is Cassie's own character — the Cassie pointer, caret eyes and cursor-state moods were made for Cassie.
