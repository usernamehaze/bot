# Learn: which labs are in which level

Each lab names its levels in its own file (`levels: [...]`). A lab is in a level only if students at that level really study its topic, at most 2 levels. Grade school is Kindergarten to Grade 6 after the little ones; High school is Grades 7 to 12 (Junior High and Senior High). LET and after graduation follows the LET General Education coverage.
