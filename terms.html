API keys and other services

Cassie uses AI. To answer you, she sends your questions, and any file, photo or voice you add, to AI services run by other companies (or to Cassie's server, which passes them on). The Privacy Policy names each one and what it is for. If you add your own Groq or Google Gemini key, your use of those services also follows their terms.

Paid plans

Cassie is free today. Paid plans are passes: one payment for a set time, and they never renew by themselves. The price and how long the pass lasts are shown right next to the buy button before you pay. Nothing is ever charged again unless you choose to buy another pass.

  • Monthly: one payment for 30 days. It never renews. We remind you in the app and by email a few days before it ends, so you can buy another month if you want.
  • A Semester pass (the Pasado Pass) is one payment and never renews. It lasts 5 months, and we tell you in the app and by email about a week before it ends. You won't be charged again unless you choose to buy another plan.
  • Yearly: one payment for 12 months. It never renews, and we remind you a month before it ends.
  • 7 days to change your mind. If you're not happy with the first payment for any plan, email askcassie.study@gmail.com within 7 days of paying and you get all of it back. No questions asked.
  • Founding price. If you buy your next pass before the one you have ends, you keep the price you started with, even if our prices go up. If you come back after it has ended, the price at that time applies.
