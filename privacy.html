Cassie — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Cassie is a free study helper — a browser extension and web app that gives you explanations, answers, and code for text you highlight. This policy explains what Cassie does and does not do with your information. In plain terms: Cassie has no account and does not sell or share your personal data. The web app counts anonymous usage (see below), and you can switch that off.

What Cassie stores on your device

  • Your API key — the free Groq API key (and, in the web app, an optional Google Gemini key for images) that you enter. It is stored only in your own browser's local storage on your device. It is never sent anywhere except directly to that provider when you ask a question.
  • Your settings — such as your chosen model.
  • Your profile (web app) — the name, grade or field of work, and age you enter when you first open Cassie. It is used only to pitch answers at the right level, stays in your browser, and can be edited in Settings → You.
  • Answer history — recent questions and answers are saved locally in your browser so you can review them. You can clear this at any time from the extension's popup. It never leaves your device.

None of this is transmitted to the developer. There is no Cassie account or login.

What is sent when you ask a question

When you highlight text (or use "Ask about this page"), the selected text — or the text of the page you chose — is sent, together with your question, directly from your browser to the AI provider so it can generate a response:

  • Groq — used for all text answers. See Groq's privacy policy.
  • Google Gemini — used only in the web app, and only for images (generating pictures or reading a photo you attach). See Google's terms.

These requests go straight from your device to the provider using your own API key. The developer of Cassie never receives, sees, or stores this content.

Anonymous usage counts (web app)

To learn how many people use Cassie and which features help most, the web app sends small anonymous counts to a stats server run by the developer:

  • a random ID made on your device (not linked to your name or email),
  • when Cassie is opened, and which feature was used (for example "chat", "quiz", "graph", "picture"),
  • broad groups only: student or working, school level (e.g. "Senior High School"), an age range (e.g. "18-24"), phone or computer, and language.

Topic words (adults only, optional). If you are 18 or older and leave "Share topic words" on, up to five keywords from a message (for example "photosynthesis") are counted so the developer can see popular subjects. Common words are removed, and nothing is collected in Talk mode. Your full messages, Cassie's answers, files, photos, your name and your exact age are never sent.

You can switch both off any time in Settings → You. The browser extension does not send usage counts.

What Cassie does NOT do

  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No advertising, and no third-party trackers — the anonymous counts above go only to Cassie's own stats server.
  • No hidden or background activity — Cassie only acts when you deliberately highlight text or click its button.
  • No collection of browsing history, keystrokes, or personal information.

Permissions

  • Access to the pages you use it on — so it can read the text you highlight and show the answer there.
  • Storage — to save your API key, settings, and answer history locally, as described above.
  • Active tab — to read the current page's text when you click "Ask about this page."

Children and intended use

Cassie is an open, visible homework and reading aid. It is not designed for use during tests, quizzes, or other graded work.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change. Continued use of Cassie means you accept the current policy.
