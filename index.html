Energy in cells

Lesson 3.2 · page 14

Every cell in your body needs energy. It gets it through cellular respiration: inside the mitochondria, glucose from your food reacts with oxygen to make ATP, and you breathe out the carbon dioxide that’s left.

Plants run it the other way. With the chlorophyll in their leaves, they use photosynthesis to turn carbon dioxide, water and sunlight into glucose, and the oxygen you breathe.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the Pasado Pass?

It’s Cassie Plus for your whole semester: 5 months for one payment of ₱599. It never renews, so nothing is charged when it ends, and we tell you in the app and by email about a week before it does. If it isn’t for you, email askcassie.study@gmail.com within 7 days of paying and you get all of it back, no questions asked.

Is Cassie really free?

Yes, forever, with no card needed: 20 questions a day, 20 quick pictures a day and 3 Gemini pictures a week, plus a taste of the 3D (2 views of each a week) and talking with Cassie. If you add your own free Groq or Gemini key, you can use Cassie as much as you like.

What can she read?

Any website you highlight or snip, PDFs, PowerPoint and Google Slides, Word files and Google Docs, and photos or screenshots of your notes, including the diagrams and graphs in them.

Does it work on my phone or tablet?

Yes. Open Cassie in your browser and add it to your home screen. For PDFs, Word and PowerPoint, attach the file and tap 📖 Read & highlight: read it inside Cassie and highlight any part. In other apps: on Android, select the words → Share → Cassie. On iPhone and iPad, set up the one-time “Ask Cassie” Shortcut (steps in Cassie → Settings → Use Cassie in other apps). Screenshots and photos of your notes work too. The on-page popup and snipping on a computer come with the Chrome extension.

How do I add Cassie to Chrome?

Download the Cassie extension, then unzip it. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked and pick the cassie-extension folder. Pin Cassie from the puzzle-piece menu and you’re done. No key needed. Works in Chrome and Edge on a computer.

Where do my notes and chats go?

They’re saved in your own browser, not on our servers. Your question and any file you attach are sent to the AI service to get an answer, and that’s it. If you make an account, these sync so you have them on every device: your profile, settings, saved quiz mistakes, flashcards, exam plans and your weekly study counts. Your chats stay on your device. You can export a backup or clear everything at any time.

Can I use Cassie during a test or quiz?

No, and please don’t. Cassie is built for studying: understanding readings, practising, and making reviewers. Using her during a graded test or quiz isn’t what she’s for.

How do I pay from the Philippines?

When paid plans launch, you’ll pay through Lemon Squeezy, Cassie’s payment partner, with a debit or credit card, Google Pay or Apple Pay. GCash and Maya aren’t offered yet; if that changes, this answer will say so. Everything on Free works today with no card.
