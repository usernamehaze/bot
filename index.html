FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Cassie really free?

Yes, forever, with no card needed: 20 questions a day, 5 file reviewers and 10 pictures a month, plus Explore 3D and talking with Cassie. If you add your own free Groq or Gemini key, you can use Cassie as much as you like.

What can she read?

Any website you highlight or snip, PDFs, PowerPoint and Google Slides, Word files and Google Docs, and photos or screenshots of your notes — including the diagrams and graphs in them.

Does it work on my phone or tablet?

Yes. Open Cassie in your browser and add it to your home screen. For PDFs, Word and PowerPoint, attach the file and tap 📖 Read & highlight: read it inside Cassie and highlight any part. In other apps: on Android, select the words → Share → Cassie. On iPhone and iPad, set up the one-time “Ask Cassie” Shortcut (steps in Cassie → Settings → Use Cassie in other apps). Screenshots and photos of your notes work too. The on-page popup and snipping on a computer come with the Chrome extension.

How do I add Cassie to Chrome?

Download the Cassie extension, then unzip it. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked and pick the cassie-extension folder. Pin Cassie from the puzzle-piece menu and you’re done — no key needed. Works in Chrome and Edge on a computer.

Where do my notes and chats go?

They’re saved in your own browser, not on our servers. Your question and any file you attach are sent to the AI service to get an answer, and that’s it. If you make an account, only your profile, settings and saved quiz mistakes sync. You can export a backup or clear everything at any time.

Can I use Cassie during a test or quiz?

No — and please don’t. Cassie is built for studying: understanding readings, practising, and making reviewers. Using her during a graded test or quiz isn’t what she’s for.

How do I pay from the Philippines?

When paid plans launch you’ll be able to pay in pesos with local options like GCash and Maya, or by card. Everyone else pays in US dollars.
