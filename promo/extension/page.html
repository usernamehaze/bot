Photosynthesis

How plants make their own food from light

Plants use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to make glucose, a sugar they use for energy. Oxygen is given off as a by-product of the light reactions in the leaves.

6CO2 + 6H2O + light → C6H12O6 + 6O2

The two stages

StageWhereWhat it makes
Light reactionsThylakoidATP, NADPH and oxygen
Calvin cycleStromaGlucose

The light reactions need sunlight. The Calvin cycle uses the ATP and NADPH they make to turn carbon dioxide into sugar.
